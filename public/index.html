Under the hood how the machine decided

The Lab

Two small experiments on the machine's brain. Every number is measured live by the server when you press the button.

01Does pruning help?

Plain minimax checks every possible future. Alpha-beta skips futures that provably can't change its answer. The move should be identical, with far less work.

02Which way of judging wins?

Five ways of scoring a position play each other. Every pairing is played on the same board twice with the sides swapped, so nobody gets a head start.
